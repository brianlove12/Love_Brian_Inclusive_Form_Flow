Telepathic Race Workshop Registration

Register for an introductory workshop exploring Telepathic Race: Secrets of the Ten Spiritual Vibrations. This workshop is intended for adults who are interested in learning about the Ten Spiritual Vibrations and discussing the ideas presented in the book.

F1 · Purpose and Expectations

This registration should take about 3–5 minutes to complete.

Fields marked “required” must be completed. Fields marked “optional” may be left blank.

This is a classroom prototype and does not send or store registration information. In a real registration system, the attendee would receive a confirmation and workshop details after submitting the form.

Privacy: Use fictional information only. Do not enter real personal, medical, financial, or other sensitive information.

F4 · Requirements and Validation

Review these requirements before completing the registration form.

  • Full name and email address are required.
  • Email must be entered in a valid email address format.
  • A workshop session and attendance method must be selected.
  • Registration requests must include between 1 and 4 attendees.
  • Accessibility requests are optional and limited to 300 characters.
  • Agreement to the workshop participation guidelines is required before submission.
